== S1: Hypothetical: video call with 'CFO' and colleagues asks a finance clerk to make urgent confidential transfers
   decision: VERIFY_FIRST
   red flag: pressure to act quickly
   red flag: asked to keep it secret or bypass normal process
   red flag: request arrived on an unofficial channel (external_video_link)
   red flag: new payee or changed payment details
   step: Do not continue on this channel: contact the person through an official channel and report the contact to security.
   step: Call back using the number in the company directory, not one given in the request.
   step: Obtain a second approver through the payment system's normal workflow.
